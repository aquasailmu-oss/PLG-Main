The Illustration Board

PRIVATE MAURITIUS

Nine ideas for hand-drawn art on the PrimaLuxGo website, shown as rough sketches in our colours: navy ink, small touches of gold, on cream. On the live site one illustrator redraws all of it in a single engraved style.

1 · Island Atlas map

Used on the Experiences page and a teaser on Home. Each place on the chart opens the activities for that area.

2 · Wildlife vignettes, animated

Small living scenes for the About page, the Journal and loading screens. Each animal does one thing: the tropicbird flies, the dodo walks, the giant tortoise eats and the pink pigeon pecks for seeds.

3 · Three arch doorways

The Home page entrance to Ocean, Island and Sky, and the header image of each collection page.

4 · Activity stamps

One emblem per activity: on the activity cards, in the filters, and as the icons in each page's "what to bring" and "good to know" lists.

5 · Wave and sun-ray dividers

Thin gold line art between page sections, and a wave texture behind the seal.

The seal with a wave textureAlso the stamp at the bottom of every page

6 · Dawn to dusk sky strip

A thin strip at the top of each activity page. As the guest scrolls through the day's timeline, the strip moves from dawn to dusk.

7 · Botanical corners

Drawn into the corners of the invitation-style cards and under section headings. Ravenala palm, hibiscus and sugar cane.

Ravenala · hibiscus · sugar cane

8 · Postcard stamp and ticket stub

The "booking confirmed" screen, the confirmation email header and the digital gift voucher.

9 · Line-drawing animation

Map routes, the seal and section dividers draw themselves in as they scroll into view. With reduced motion switched on, they simply appear.

Sketches only, to show the direction. Names such as Le Morne and Chamarel are real places; the chart shape is simplified and not to scale.
